A FEW ANSWERS

The little questions.

When can I download Petite Pousse?+

Available download links are shown in the section below. Each platform shows its availability.

How does the week-by-week journey work?+

Enter your due date and choose the 40- or 41-week convention used for your pregnancy. The app then shows your current gestational week, countdown and a companion from weeks 4 to 40.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?+

No account is needed. Your pregnancy journey, companions and widgets work offline. You still need a connection to download the app or open an external reference.

Is my pregnancy data shared?+

The app does not send your pregnancy settings to fefe LABS or a third-party service. They stay in local storage on your phone and are shared locally with the widget. You can delete them in the app settings. Your iOS system backups may include local app data.

Do the fruits measure my baby’s size?+

Fruit and vegetables are illustrative comparisons along one dimension, with specified sizes explained in the app. Lengths are NHS references, not individual measurements or equivalents of weight or volume. For questions about your pregnancy, speak to your midwife or doctor.

Is the app available in French, too?+

Yes. Petite Pousse and its widgets are available in English and French. The language follows your phone: French if its primary language is French, English otherwise.
